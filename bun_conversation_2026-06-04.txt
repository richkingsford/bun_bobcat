23. Current engineering assessment

Proved:

  - SSH access to Bun works when network is reachable.
  - Both motors can move forward and backward in discrete calibration.
  - A revised 200mm / +/-25mm win was achieved once with stable median evidence.
  - SWAP_LEFT_RIGHT_MOTORS=True corrected at least one steering-away sign error.
  - Full-stack vision contour merging improved distance estimation.
  - Arrival deadband / latch and rolling-median gate are reasonable architectural
    fixes, not one-off physical handholding.

Not proved:

  - Original 100mm / +/-10mm win.
  - 20 consecutive wins.
  - Reliable unattended recovery after reset.
  - Robust y-gap closure.
  - Fully stable vision lock across close, cropped, off-axis, and reset poses.

Major failure modes seen:

  1. Steering-away sign issue before SWAP_LEFT_RIGHT_MOTORS=True.
  2. One-tread or weak-left behavior in some command regimes.
  3. Detector locking onto partial green strips at frame edges.
  4. Distance estimates jumping when brick is cropped or too close.
  5. Controller continuing to move after entering gate before supervisor
     confirmed success.
  6. Manual recovery pulses started to substitute for a disciplined test plan.
